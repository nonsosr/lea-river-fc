The club

Lea River FC is a Sunday league side formed in 2026, playing in Hackney & Leyton Sunday Football League Division 3. We take our name and our badge from the river that runs through this corner of east London.

Home games are played at Hackney Marshes. We are a new club with a new squad, and we would rather have a crowd than not — everyone is welcome on the touchline.
